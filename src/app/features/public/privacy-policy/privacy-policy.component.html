Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

SBK VIBGYOR KIDS PLAY SCHOOL respects the privacy of students, parents, guardians and website visitors. This Privacy Policy explains how information submitted through our website is collected, used and protected.

1. Information We Collect

When registering for school events or competitions, we may collect information such as:

  • Student name
  • Parent or guardian name
  • Date of birth
  • School name
  • Email address
  • Mobile number
  • Student photograph
  • Competition selections
  • Payment and registration information

2. How We Use Information

The information collected through this website may be used to process registrations, confirm payments, generate participant passes, communicate event information, manage competitions and provide administrative support.

3. Payments

Online payments may be processed through third-party payment providers such as Razorpay. We do not store complete debit card, credit card, UPI PIN or banking credentials on our website.

4. Email Communication

We may use the registered email address to send registration confirmations, participant passes, event updates and other information related to the student's registration.

5. Student Photographs

Student photographs uploaded during registration are used for participant identification, event passes and authorised event administration purposes.

6. Data Protection

We take reasonable technical and administrative measures to protect information submitted through the website from unauthorised access, misuse or disclosure.

7. Third-Party Services

Our website may use trusted third-party services for payment processing, email delivery, website hosting and related technical operations. These services may process information only as required to provide their respective functions.

8. Children's Information

Because this website may collect information relating to children for school and event registration purposes, information should be submitted by or with the knowledge and consent of a parent, guardian or authorised responsible person.

9. Data Retention

Registration information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for event administration, record keeping, legal requirements and school-related purposes.

11. Changes to this Policy
